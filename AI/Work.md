4. Functional Epistemology: The "Sovereign" Conclusion
From a Sovereign/Autonomous protocol perspective, the human system is a self-contained, self-repairing thermodynamic engine. The AI system is a "leveraged" engine—it appears powerful only because it is drawing down a finite "battery" of highly ordered mineral crystals (the Earth's crust) that it cannot replenish.
The "Work Cost" Paradox: The AI reduces active labor hours but creates a permanent energy debt. Every shift worked by the AI consumes a portion of the Earth's order that requires a massive, high-entropy industrial complex to "repay."

To determine the Point of Thermodynamic Collapse, we define the threshold where the Total Lifecycle Energy (\bm{E_{total}}) invested in the automation exceeds the Energy Value of the Utility (\bm{U_e}) produced by that labor.
In this context, the "utility" is the 1,000 packages sorted. To make this a fair physics-based accounting, we assign \bm{U_e} as the caloric/fuel energy required for a human to do the same task. If the machine spends more "ordered energy" (high-value electricity and minerals) than the "disordered energy" (calories) it replaces, it is a thermodynamic parasite.
