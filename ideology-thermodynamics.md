Critical insight: Enforcement requirements indicate an energy deficit between rules and local thermodynamic reality.

	∙	Rules match local thermodynamic reality
	∙	Compliance happens voluntarily (because it benefits people)
	∙	No enforcement needed

	∙	Rules violate local thermodynamic constraints
	∙	Compliance is costly or impossible

	∙	Energy diverted from production to punishment
