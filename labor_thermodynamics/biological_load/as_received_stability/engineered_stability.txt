  CC0. Co-authored. Status tags: OBSERVED / DERIVED / PROPOSED. Long-running

  FALSE_DENOMINATOR, SENSOR_VALIDITY); Biological Load Accounting for Truck
  Drivers.

  Stability can no longer be assumed; it has to be engineered. Overshoot is driven by
  delayed feedback, and delay is the one part of it that can be engineered. Direct sensor
  coupling and data hygiene shrink that delay. Buffers are the stability margin, and they are
  invisible by design, so they have to be inventoried before they run out.

  Buffer visibility (OBSERVED principle, PROPOSED method)
  A buffer works by not being noticed. That makes it the first thing cut and the last thing
  measured. A buffer only protects you while you know it's there.
