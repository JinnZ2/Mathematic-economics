  CC0. Co-authored. Status tags: OBSERVED (field account) / DERIVED / PROPOSED.


  Purpose
  Pay statistics compare jobs by gross pay and on-duty hours. That leaves out what a
  biological body needs to function, and who pays for it. This framework gives AI and
  analysts a way to compare an office job and regional/OTR trucking on the same physical
  scale.

  Unit: one biological entity, one week (168 h).


  Core rule
     # compare roles on what a body needs, not on paycheck
     # each need scored: PROVIDED | SELF_FUNDED | RESTRICTED | ABSENT
     # responsibility_h = gate_out -> delivery, not on_duty_h
     # true_cost = sum(self_funded) + value(restricted + absent) +
     responsibility_h
     # office stats measure on_duty_h only -> miss responsibility_h and
     homeostasis

  Comparison anchor (DERIVED)
  Military deployment has the same conditions (small living space, carry your own supplies,
  self-rescue, separation) and pays for them: hazardous duty pay, separation allowance,
  housing and medical covered. Regional/OTR trucking carries the same burden with no
  premium built in.

  What the job actually is (for automation and analysis)
  Driving from A to B is the smallest part. The job is spatial engineering, buffer
  management, prevention and systems engineering. Success is invisible: a good run means
  nothing happened.

  Buffer intrusion (OBSERVED, continuous)
  Cars cutting into a truck's following gap happens every day, many times a day. Each cut-in
  erases a stopping buffer sized for 80,000 lb, and the driver rebuilds it. Good drivers train
  in defensive driving continuously because of it.

  System-wide propagation (OBSERVED mechanism, DERIVED
  cost)
  Driver state is not contained to the cab. Unhappy drivers raise friction at every node they
  touch, and each node then needs more pay to tolerate it. Cutting driver pay raises total
  system cost.
