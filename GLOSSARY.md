## How to use this glossary

For each term used in this repository's documentation or code, the
academic equivalent gives you:

- A pointer to the established literature where the concept is
  studied under a different name;
- A citation handle for peer-reviewed engagement;
- A check that the concept is not being invented from scratch — most
  substrate-primary terms have established academic shadows.

The repository's terms are not different concepts; they are *renamed
to resist the projection errors* that the academic terms carry from
their disciplinary contexts. Both vocabularies point at the same
substrate.
